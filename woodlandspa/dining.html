Dietary Requirements

Allergen information

We cannot accommodate dietary requirements at the Terrace Bar. Within Bertram’s Restaurant and Rooftop Bar, we accommodate the 14 main allergens. Notify us 48 hours in advance for requirements outside these.

Bertram’s Restaurant

2 AA Rosette dining

Our award-winning spa restaurant has been completely transformed — a destination in its own right. Season-led Lancashire produce, an extensive wine list, and a dedicated vegan menu. Your spa day includes a main course, dessert and glass of wine.

Tapas in the Terrace

Relaxed terrace dining

Share small plates on our retractable-roof terrace — from keema lamb samosas to gambas pil pil. Available 12pm to 7.30pm, with a special offer of two tapas and a Prosecco cocktail for £22.

Pizza on the Rooftop

Stone-baked under open skies

Twelve-inch pizzas from our Cuppone stone-bake oven, naturally leavened with Italian ’00’ flour. The south-facing Rooftop is the place to be when the sun shines — stonebaked pizza, cocktails, and fire pit loungers with views over Lancashire.
