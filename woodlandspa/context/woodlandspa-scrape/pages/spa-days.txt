The Woodland Spa, Lancashire, provides the perfect sanctuary in which to indulge yourself with spa days which provide unmatched levels of luxury.

All our Spa Days include unlimited access to the Thermal Experience for the entire day of your visit

Our Spa Day collections also include opportunities to complete your sensory journey with a unique dining experience in our award-winning 2-Rosette Spa Restaurant – where the food you will eat actually tastes as good as it is good for you.

We also have a range of indulgent spa days which include a luxury treatment delivered in the most beautifully appointed spa treatment rooms by highly trained therapists who provide you with their undivided attention.

So whether you choose a spa day with or without treatments, you will enjoy unmatched levels of relaxation which are to be savoured…
