The Thermal Spa Experience at The Woodland Spa in Lancashire is like no other. This unique journey through heat and ice will soothe, relax and detoxify.

Visit Us
YOUR THERMAL JOURNEYWith its fabulous brand-new £16 million upgraded facilities

Hydrotherapy Pool – Feel the stress melt away as powerful massage jets work the whole body to eliminate muscular tension and improve circulation
Serenity Pool – Escape into this warm low-lit relaxation room with a high-temperature pool and let your stress float away.
Outdoor Infinity Pool – Enter this unique 12-metre heated pool from the inside pool deck, swim outside and take in the fresh air. Then hop over to one of our spacious outdoor hot tubs. What could be more exhilarating?
Outdoor Terrace – with enough loungers to go round and covered by a retractable roof for inclement weather. we ensure you can enjoy an outdoor experience even if it rains.
Outdoor Infinity Edge Spa Jacuzzis – Two huge 16-seater Jacuzzis ensure there’s plenty of room to fully destress, relax and take in the great British outdoor air.
Fire Pit – Kick back and relax with a cocktail in our fire pit lounge.
How comfy are those sofas!
Terrace Bar & Lounge – Enjoy a selection of the most delectable tapas dishes and a cocktail. It’d be rude not to!
Sauna – The most traditional of thermal rooms, this high-temperature dry heat induces total muscle relaxation
Saunarium – Similar to a traditional sauna but with less intense heat, this unique outdoor thermal room allows you more time to relax and take in the view
Steam – With virtually 100% humidity this “softer” heat is less drying on the skin, and provides stimulation to the blood circulation whilst you relax
Salt Steam – Salt and steam combine to create a sea atmosphere, helping you breathe more easily, relax and clear the mind
Roman Foot Baths – Intense bubbles offer a reflexology massage to the feet, stimulating every pressure point
Ice Fountain – Based upon the old Finnish idea of rolling in the snow after taking a sauna, this modern equivalent is here to rapidly cool the body by rubbing crushed ice onto the skin after an intense heat experience
Experience Showers—Around the hydro pool, you can find a variety of different showers, from soft tropical rain to an ice-cold bucket shower!
Rasul – Within our thermal suite we have a 2/4 person Rasul Mud Chamber.
Organic mineral mud is applied to the body before relaxing in a traditional steam room.
Then let the mud deep cleanse, exfoliate and nourish the skin before the gentle rain shower washes it away. (Chargeable treatment – read more here)
BRAND-NEW EXPERIENCES 
Vitality Pool – This dramatic Vitality Pool boasts no less than four spacious hot tubs, two of which are elevated with an infinity edge, here you’ll find water canons, massage stations and a gentle waterfall rain shower
Ergonomic Heated Loungers – accessed via the heated poolside promenade and perfect for resting upon after your hydrotherapy experience
The Mezzanine – our fabulous mezzanine is the epitome of luxury relaxation; it features a variety of bespoke relaxation areas with sensory lighting. It’s the perfect place to lie back and have that much-needed catch-up conversation
 Ice Lounge – This world-leading, multi-sensory KLAFS ice lounge is the UK’s first – exhilarating!
Roman Foot Baths – features foot reflexology massage. Sit back, relax, and let the spa world pass you by
The Rooftop – given its south-facing position, there’s nowhere better to be when the sun shines. And it’s warm up here even when you think the sun’s not out! When you’ve finished relaxing on your sun lounger, consider heading over to one of two comfortable outdoor firepit lounges or head into the Rooftop Bar for some comforting food and a refreshing cocktail. When the conditions allow, its retractable roof and windows ensure that you don’t miss out on any Vitamin D and allow you to make the most of the sun’s rays
UK’s Longest Infinity Edge Spa Pool – Oh the views! Go and see for yourself…
Spa robes, spa flip flops and towels will be provided.

Opening Hours8.30am – 9.30pm
Please note the changing areas will remain open for a further 30 minutes after the above changing times

You won’t want it to end.
