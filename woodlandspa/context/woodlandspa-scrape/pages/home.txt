Welcome to The Woodland Spa
The Best Day Spa in the UK – not just in Lancashire

The Woodland Spa is arguably positioned to be the UK’s largest luxury spa destination, yet it remains intimately beautiful.

No longer must you wait to experience a luxury spa escape like no other  – we’ve unveiled our additional brand-new £16 million thermal experiences.
Our magnificent spa has expanded – doubled in size – and we’ve introduced even more beautiful thermal experiences. However, we understand and value the luxury of space, and therefore we will not double the number of guests we invite each day to share in the splendour of it all. This will ensure your visit is as tranquil as it is exclusive. Furthermore, we don’t limit your time in our experiences to just two hours. When you visit us, you may stay for the entirety of your day. Luxurious spa relaxation mustn’t be a rushed experience.

The first thing you’ll notice when you visit our spa in Lancashire is our location. Set within 100 acres of the most magnificent Lancashire countryside with its undulating rolling landscape, sensational woodland and lush verdant pasture, there is a true sense of intimacy and peace created by the surroundings. The dramatic, distinct landscape is dominated by the impressive Pendle Hill – famous for its tales of Lancashire witches, sorcery and black magic in the 17th-century.
As you make your way down our winding lane, you will find yourself unwinding. At the end of your visit, you’ll find yourself wondering how soon you can return…

Whether you choose to visit just for The Experience, indulge in one of our luxury Spa Days which includes a delicious treatment, or better still enjoy the most gratifying experience of all – a Spa Break, we’re certain you’ll love it here. Step out of your world and into ours…
Prepare to nourish your body, mind and soul
