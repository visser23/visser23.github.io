Booking Request Form
We’re already looking forward to making your spa experience a special one.
We will contact you by telephone to confirm availability as soon as possible. Should we be unable to contact you in the first instance by telephone, we will proceed to email you. Rest assured, your privacy is important to us and we will not use your details for any other reason.

How to find usThe Woodland Spa in Burnley is located just off junction 11 of the M65 making it very easily accessible from Blackburn, Accrington, Nelson and Colne.

From the M65 Motorway Eastbound
from Blackburn:

The Woodland Spa is situated just off junction 11 of M65. However there is no junction 11 exit when travelling eastbound.
Therefore we recommend that you exit M65 at junction 12 eastbound and then travel westbound to exit at junction 11.
Leave the motorway at Junction 12, signposted Nelson & Brierfield
Take the 4th exit off the roundabout onto M65 Westbound
Leave the motorway at Junction 11, signposted Burnley
Take 3rd exit onto Holme Road, signposted for Crow Wood Resort

From the M65 Motorway Westbound
from Colne/ Pendle:

Leave the motorway at Junction 11, signposted Burnley.
Take 3rd exit onto Holme Road, signposted for Crow Wood Resort
