We’ve embraced our passion for exquisite freshly prepared food, beautifully presented and served in the most unique of surroundings…
Our very beautiful Spa Restaurant has recently undergone a complete transformation, so why not take in the sumptuous interior of our unique restaurant whilst enjoying your delicious spa menu.
Alternatively, for a more relaxed, casual dining experience you may enjoy pizza on the Rooftop Restaurant or kick back and enjoy Tapas in the Terrace Bar.
Wherever you choose the dine, we promise you a tasteful experience.
Ask our Spa Reservations Team to book a table for you.
Got any questions? Visit our FAQs and find the answer.
Please be aware that we cannot accommodate food allergies, intolerances, or celiac disease at the Terrace Bar. However, within Bertram’s Restaurant and our Rooftop Bar, we can accommodate guests with the 14 main allergens. If you have an allergy that falls outside of these, please notify us at least 48 hours in advance, as a pre-order may be necessary. 
These menus are illustrative of the types of dishes available and are subject to change seasonally and at any time.

Pizza on the Rooftop Menu
Gourmet Stonebaked PizzaAll our pizzas are 12” round, made with Italian “00” flour and cooked in our Cuppone stone-bake oven. 
A naturally leavened, long fermentation process does all the hard work for us, breaking down the gluten and starch, delivering a highly digestible, low G.I. bread which is much easier on the body.
Our finished pizzas are soft & chewy in parts, blistered and cracked in others, leaving an excellent crust not to be discarded…

Cereals Containing Gluten: Pizzas can be created using a gluten-free pizza base on request at a supplement of £2.
