Job Title: Front of house – Spa Receptionist
Full and Part Time roles available
It’s important to note that this role involves working on a rota which includes extended hours and some Saturdays and Sundays.
We are seeking an individual who has a passion for customer care and a keen interest in the spa and beauty industry! An enthusiastic individual who will always go the extra mile.
You will be working alongside our existing spa receptionists and become part of our Spa family.
Fulfilling the role as Spa Receptionist you will play a vital role in the running of our award-winning spa as you will be the first and last face all our guests and members see.
As Spa Receptionist you will be encouraged to deliver excellent customer service to ensure our guests have an unforgettable experience from the moment they arrive.
Previous receptionist experience is sought after, along with strong administrational skills. A background in retail is beneficial as developing the knowledge of the products sold in our Spa boutique will be required.
The successful candidate will be required to:

Assist guests on a day to day basis; welcoming them on arrival and ensuring a smooth guest experience at our Spa Reception.
Assist with inbound telephone and email enquiries for reservations, using multiple booking systems
Work different shift patterns within our opening hours including alternate weekends

Essential attributes:

Excellent communication and people skills
The ability to work effectively in a demanding environment
Ability to work on their own initiative within a work environment
Excellent computer skills
Excellent personal presentation

Industry experience would be preferred but is not essential. Experience in a similar customer facing role would be an advantage but full training will be provided.
The Woodland Spa reception operates 7 days a week between 6.30am and 10pm
To apply, please send a covering letter along with your CV to:
karen@thewoodlandspa.com
Closing date for applications: 7th June 2019
Previous applicants need not apply
