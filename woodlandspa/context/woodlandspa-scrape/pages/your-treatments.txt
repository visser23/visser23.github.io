Luxury Treatments

Our luxury treatments are delivered in equally luxurious of surroundings here at The Woodland Spa. Whether you visit one of our beautifully appointed treatment rooms or you indulge in a very special hand or foot treatment at one of our fabulous luxury pedicure stations or nail bars – you’re sure to enjoy your treatments.
Our highly trained and experienced therapists are on hand to answer any questions you might have about skincare or suitable home care products.
You are invited to enjoy our treatments safely and confidently if you are living with cancer. All our therapists have received full training from Amethyst Trust. The training is approved by SATCC, Authority for Touch in Cancer Care which was set up to regulate the training of cancer massage.

Spa Treatment Information
It is essential that you advise us of any medical conditions and allergies prior to arrival and receiving your beauty treatment so that we can ensure treatments are suitable for you. To allow you to relax fully before your luxury treatment, we ask that you arrive 15 minutes prior to your treatment time. Arriving late for your appointment may result in your treatment being cut short, as timings for subsequent clients have to be maintained. All treatment timings include consultation and aftercare advice. Please give us 24 hours notice if you are unable to make your appointment and we will be happy to reschedule. Otherwise, we reserve the right to charge the full amount of the treatment. For your enjoyment, the Spa is an adult only facility (16 years and over).
Got any questions? Visit our FAQs and find the answer.
