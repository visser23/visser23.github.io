If you want your skin to look at its best then you must take care of it. Our experienced and expertly trained professional TEMPLESPA & Medik8 therapists will be happy to advise on the facial that will best suit your skin’s needs. And why shouldn’t you enjoy yourself in the process? Our beautifully appointed treatment rooms provide the most opulent of surroundings in which to fully relax and immerse yourself in the beauty of it all. Look your best, feel your best – with a facial treatment at The Woodland Spa.

TEMPLESPA Champagne & Truffles Deluxe Facial
75 minutes | £117
A luxury facial that combines our Award-Winning truffle skincare with our specialist lifting massage techniques that together iron out deep lines and wrinkles, help to strengthen muscle tone and reduce puffiness. You’ll have something to celebrate.

Medik8 Crystal Retinal Age-Defying Facial
75 minutes | £117
Our most rejuvenating facial, powered by Crystal Retinal and enhanced with our transformative sculpting massage. This facial is a truly bespoke experience and tailored to meet your skin’s unique needs. Designed to deliver visible results in just 1 treatment, it helps to reduce the appearance of wrinkles, boost radiance, and improve skin firmness.

Medik8 Expert Facial
60 minutes | £104
Crafted by your Medik8 Professional for a truly personalised experience, this treatment nourishes deeply, enhances skin clarity, and promotes a smoother, more even complexion. Complete with a soothing yet invigorating massage, it rejuvenates both skin and senses for the perfect restorative reset.

TEMPLESPA My Kinda Skin Prescription Facial
60 minutes | £104
Let us analyse your skin and prepare a tailored treatment just for you. Your facial includes a tailored face and décolleté massage, plus a relaxing scalp massage. You will feel amazing and your skin will look so, so good.

30 minutes | £62
