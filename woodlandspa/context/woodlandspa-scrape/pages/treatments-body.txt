Exfoliating Body Treatments

Our luxury body treatments soothe away every bit of tension. Our body treatments are expertly delivered by highly experienced, trained therapists and in our beautifully appointed treatment rooms. Feel at your best – with a soothing body treatment.

The Total Glow Up
Glow & Radiance Face & Body Experience
90 minutes | £148
This unique face and body treatment leaves your skin, face and body positively glowing from top-to-toe!
To begin, you’ll be treated to a body brush, scrub, and application of moisturiser, followed by a face and body contouring treatment which works on relieving tension and improving tone. Lastly, our advanced skin-transforming facial turns up your glow with instant and visible results.

Paradise Escape
105 minutes | £168
Indulge yourself in this ultimate body experience. After our aromatic foot ritual, your body will be exfoliated to restore radiance and ensure the softest, smoothest skin. A relaxing full body massage, with pure essential oils, ensures your skin is completely nourished. Your hands and feet will be enveloped with a warm, comforting mask whilst you enjoy a blissful neck and scalp massage. A truly exquisite experience!

NEOM Real Luxury Body Scrub
75 minutes | £117
Combine skin nourishment and rich exfoliation with a cocooning blend of relaxing essential oils, including lavender, jasmine & sandalwood. Following a combination of thoughtful massage techniques, you will be left feeling rejuvenated with skin that’s soft, smooth and luxuriously hydrated.

TEMPLESPA Golden Truffle Experience
60 minutes | £104
This indulgent treatment combines an invigorating, radiance-boosting body exfoliation followed by a skin-transforming treat with our luxurious Truffle Massage Oil and a layer of our unique, award-winning Body Truffle Crème. Skin is left feeling cashmere soft and revived, beautifully fragrant and ready for the red carpet.

TEMPLESPA Back To Life
45 minutes | £69
This invigorating treat includes an exfoliation scrub, a deep cleanse with warm purifying mud followed by a stimulating massage. Leaves you with a back to be proud of.

NEOM Reviving Back Ritual
45 minutes | £69
Treat those hard-to-reach areas with an amazing exfoliation in preparation for a wonderfully warm aromatic back, neck, and scalp massage.
