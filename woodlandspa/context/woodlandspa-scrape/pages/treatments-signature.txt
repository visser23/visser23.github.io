Signature Treatments

Our Signature Treatments have been created by us, especially for you. You won’t find these bespoke treatments anywhere else. They have been crafted to our exacting standards, using the finest products and delivering unmatched levels of relaxation and luxury.

The Total Glow Up
Glow & Radiance Face & Body Experience
90 minutes | £148
This unique face and body treatment leaves your skin, face and body positively glowing from top-to-toe!
To begin, you’ll be treated to a body brush, scrub, and application of moisturiser, followed by a face and body contouring treatment which works on relieving tension and improving tone. Lastly, our advanced skin-transforming facial turns up your glow with instant and visible results.

Paradise Escape
105 minutes | £168
Indulge yourself in this ultimate body experience. After our aromatic foot ritual, your body will be exfoliated to restore radiance and ensure the softest, smoothest skin. A relaxing full body massage, with pure essential oils, ensures your skin is completely nourished. Your hands and feet will be enveloped with a warm, comforting mask whilst you enjoy a blissful neck and scalp massage. A truly exquisite experience!
