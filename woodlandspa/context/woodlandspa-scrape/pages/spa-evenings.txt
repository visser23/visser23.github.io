SPA EVENINGS AT THE WOODLAND SPA The Twilight Experience

Unwind into the evening with Twilight Thermal Access at The Woodland Spa, Lancashire – a beautifully relaxed way to experience our acclaimed thermal suite as the day draws to a close.
From approximately 5:30 pm until 9:30 pm, this evening-only access invites you to enjoy the spa’s soothing journey through heat, ice and water in a more intimate, laid-back atmosphere.
Immerse yourself in the spa’s luxurious facilities – from steam rooms and saunas to hydrotherapy pools, serene relaxation spaces and invigorating ice features – all designed to soothe tired muscles, calm the mind and detoxify the body.
Whether you’re finishing the working day or simply craving a quieter spa experience, Twilight Thermal Access is the perfect way to escape, reset and recharge.

We’re currently trialling online bookings, available exclusively for Twilight Spa Experiences booked at least 72 hours in advance.
For last-minute availability, please call 01282 471913
