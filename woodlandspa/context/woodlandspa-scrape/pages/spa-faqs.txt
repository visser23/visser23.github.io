Spa FAQ’s

What is provided for my Thermal Experience?Within our fabulous changing facilities, you will have your personal locker equipped with a bathrobe, towel and flip flops to use for the day. We also provide hairdryers for your convenience.

Is there a time limit on using the Thermal Experience?We recommend allowing yourself at least three hours to complete the Thermal Experience journey; however, you are welcome to stay as long as you wish.

Do we need to book in advance?We recommend that you book as far in advance as possible for all thermal experience, spa days, spa treatments or spa breaks to avoid disappointment.

Do you cater for group bookings?In order to maintain the ambience, you would expect from a spa, we limit group bookings to a maximum of eight people per group. We only accept one large group booking per day. For large groups wishing to dine, our restaurants may ask for a pre-order.

Can I book online?We believe in the personal touch and want to make sure that you enjoy your experience with us; we offer such a variety of packages and treatments that’s why we prefer it if you call to book.  It also means we can let you know exactly what we can provide, suggest treatments to suit you and your guests and recommend different packages that you might not already know about; so that we can help to create the perfect spa visit for you. Don’t have time to call us? Simply fill out our booking request form under the ‘Visit Us’ tab

I have a medical condition, am I still ok to visit?We ask that you seek advice from your medical practitioner prior to your visit, to ensure they are happy for you to still use our facilities and enjoy treatments. It is essential that you advise us of any medical conditions, intolerances or allergies prior to arrival so that we can ensure everything is suitable for you. Please don’t hesitate to ask our advice when you make your booking.

Can I visit if I’m pregnant?We encourage all pregnant guests to seek advice from their medical practitioner prior to their visit to ensure they are happy to still use our facilities and enjoy our treatments. Every pregnancy is different, and some guests may be suffering other medical issues due to their pregnancy. Our reservations team can offer further advice on booking.

Can I visit if I am living with cancer?Yes, please do. We encourage all guests with medical conditions to seek advice from their medical practitioner prior to their visit, to ensure they are happy for you to still use our facilities and enjoy our treatments. All our therapists have received full training from Amethyst Trust. The course enables therapists to perform treatments on clients living with cancer, safely and confidently. The training is approved by SATCC, Authority for Touch in Cancer Care, set up to regulate the training of cancer massage.

Are there any age restrictions?The Woodland Spa is reserved for the use of adults, 16 years and over.

When do I have to pay?Full payment is required on booking for all treatments, spa days and spa breaks

I have booked a treatment, am I able to use the Thermal Experiences too?Yes, you can; however, there is an additional cost for using the Thermal Experience facilities. Visit our Spa Days page for current prices.  Booking is essential and subject to availability.

Do you have a cancellation policy?Please provide us with 24 hours’ notice if you are unable to make your appointment/spa package and we will be happy to re-schedule. Otherwise we reserve the right to charge the full amount.

Do you have Wi-Fi?Yes, we do. Whilst on the internet, why not share your spa experience via Google+ and tag us on Facebook & Instagram

Do you allow smoking in the Spa areas?Smoking (inc. E Cigarettes) is not permitted in any of our indoor or outdoor thermal areas. A designated smoking area is provided at the bottom of Bertram’s lift and also adjacent to the outdoor terrace washroom facilities.

Where can I park?Free car parking is available at our resort, our main car park for the spa is situated at the front of Crow Wood Health Club and the Spa Reception entrance.

Food & Drink FAQ’s

Is food and drink included in my Spa Day?Some of our spa packages include food and/or beverages; you can also purchase food or drinks from Bertram’s Restaurant, Terrace Bar, or Rooftop Bar during your visit. Water fountains are available inside the Thermal Suite; please stay hydrated while enjoying the facilities.

Can we bring our own food and drink?Unfortunately not, if there is any particular reason you need to do this, such as medical conditions please let us know and we will check with the Restaurant that they can cater for your needs.

Is alcohol allowed in the Spa?Water is only allowed inside the Thermal Suite, changing rooms or outdoor pools/hot tubs. Alcohol may only be consumed within Bertram’s Restaurant, Terrace Bar, Rooftop Bar or any outdoor seating areas.

Do you cater for food allergies/dietary requirements?Yes, our restaurant menus offer dishes created using gluten-free ingredients and vegetarian and vegan options. Please let us know of any allergies or special dietary requirements on booking, as you may be asked to pre-order 48 hours prior to your arrival. Please note that we cannot cater for special dietary requirements in our Terrace Tapas Bar or Rooftop Bar.

What do I need to wear for dining in the restaurant?It is possible to dine in the restaurant in your spa robes until 6pm after which time you will need to be fully dressed. There is no formal dress code for the restaurant. If you choose to dine or enjoy drinks in the Terrace Bar, robes must be worn at all times.

Accommodation FAQ’s

What type of accommodation do you have?We have a fabulous, luxury 76 bedroomed hotel just a short walk from The Woodland Spa within the Crow Wood Resort. The hotel is set in 40 acres of private parkland. With spectacular views of Pendle Hill and the surrounding countryside, a beautiful ornamental lake and fabulous gardens, it is a most wonderful place in which to extend your spa visit and create the ultimate  spacation.
We invite you to take a look at the rooms and suites which are available
Take me to Crow Wood Hotel & Spa Resort website .

What time do we need to check in and check out?Check in is from 3pm and departure is 11am.

Is the accommodation attached to the Spa?Our resort hotel is located just a short walk away. Upon checking into the hotel, you will be directed to The Woodland Spa, which is just a short walk away. Alternatively, you can request to be taken by our resort buggy. We have transport and assistance available for those who require it.

Do you allow pets?Pets are not allowed in the facility with the exception of guide dogs.

Do I get to use the spa thermal experience if I stay over?Our Thermal Experience needs to be pre-booked in advance, direct with the Spa Reservations Team, Not all packages include use of these facilities, so if you are unsure, please contact our Spa Reservations Team for further information.
Use of Crow Wood’s leisure facilities (pool & gym) are however included free of charge for all guests staying in Crow Wood Hotel.
