Your Thermal Journey

Soothe, relax and detoxify

The Thermal Spa Experience at The Woodland Spa is a unique journey through heat and ice. Drift from powerful hydrotherapy to still, warm water; from high-temperature saunas to the UK’s first multi-sensory KLAFS ice lounge. There is no schedule and no rush — your thermal suite is yours for the entire day.
