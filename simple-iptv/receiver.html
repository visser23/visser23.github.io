Simple IPTV TV receiver

Paste an HTTP/HTTPS stream URL or open a receiver link from the player. Press Play with your TV remote. Nothing is saved here.

Ready.

TV codecs and webOS versions vary. HTTPS pages can block HTTP streams; HLS fallback needs CORS and internet access to load hls.js. DRM and provider connection limits still apply. If playback fails, use an IPTV app on the TV, a compatible AirPlay route or an external receiver. Close this browser when finished.
