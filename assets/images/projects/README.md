# Story images

Local copies of Matt Visser’s project artwork, retrieved 15 October 2026.

- `prophetai.png`: screenshot of https://prophetai.club/, captured 15 October 2026. Shows the public picks and simulated-bankroll interface, replacing the social-preview artwork. PNG, 1200 × 630, matching the previous image. The figures and open positions are a dated snapshot, not current results.
- `slideclark.webp`: https://slideclark.com/img/editor.webp (the live site’s editor screenshot).
- `semantic-browser.png`: https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dac79ee0-6ebb-48b3-a27d-2e339ea16961 (mascot linked by https://github.com/visser23/semantic-browser/blob/main/README.md).
- `yap.png`: isolated browser rendering of the notes panel from the “Notes from a transcript” product illustration in https://visser23.github.io/yap/ (`#row`, second figure), captured 15 October 2026. This is the published website’s illustrative UI, not a recording of a private meeting or a native-app screenshot. Rendered with the published site’s CSS, without carousel clipping or surrounding navigation. Centred on a dark background and saved as an optimised 1024 × 1024 PNG, matching the previous icon’s dimensions. The public https://github.com/visser23/yap-releases repository contains only icon artwork, so the meaningful published product illustration was preferred. The existing `yap/icon.png` remains unchanged.

ProphetAI and Yap artwork belongs to Matt’s own projects and is reproduced here at his request. Their public sources do not state an open-source artwork licence; this reuse does not grant a third-party licence. ProphetAI’s web repositories are private, so only its public website was used. No third-party stock imagery was introduced.

Project descriptions were checked against those sites, https://visser23.github.io/yap/ and the public Semantic Browser and Yap READMEs. Launch months and the OpenClaw inspiration were supplied by Matt; the public sources do not independently establish them. Semantic Browser has no supplied launch month, so its card retains a year-only date alongside the existing year-only 2026 entry, before the dated launches.
